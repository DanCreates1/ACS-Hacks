^ what is this

Build Something
That Matters

AssumptionHacks is Windsor's first high school hackathon, a 3 day after-school event where students come together to design, build, and present projects that solve real problems. No experience required. Just curiosity, creativity, and a willingness to try.


You’ll have access to mentors, workshops, food and drinks each day, and a chance to compete for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place prizes. Teams can have up to 3 people, and solo builders are welcome too.

Build After School

FAQ

Who can participate?
Any high school student can apply. You do not need to be from Assumption, but spots are limited and preference may go to students who complete the form early.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Beginners are welcome. We’re planning beginner-friendly workshops, mentor support, and time to learn as you build.
How do teams work?
You can work solo or in a team of up to 3 people. If you do not have a team yet, there will be time to meet people and form one at the start.
What should I bring?
Bring your laptop, charger, and anything specific you want for your project. Food and drinks will be provided each day.
When does the event run?
The current plan is Tuesday to Thursday, June 2 to 4, 2026, from 2:20 PM to 6:00 PM each day. It is an after-school event, not an overnight event.
Is it free to attend?
Yes. The event is completely free for students thanks to sponsors and community support.
